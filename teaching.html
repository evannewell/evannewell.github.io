Teaching

Instructor for ECON 315 – Sports Economics (Spring 2025)

I designed this course to use ideas and examples from college and professional sports to introduce students to three major fields in economics:

  1. Industrial Organization: We discussed market power, competition, and strategic pricing in the market for sports tickets. We also covered examples of antitrust challenges in sports and ways to measure competitive balance in sports leagues.
  2. Public Economics: We explored how cities compete with each other to attract sports teams and major events. We talked about how local taxes, municipal bonds, and economic spillovers are used to fund and justify public investment in stadiums and bids to host sports events.
  3. Financial Economics: We used the growing popularity of sports betting and prediction markets to teach lessons on trading, probability, expected value, arbitrage, and adverse selection as well as the role of exchanges and market makers in modern financial markets.
