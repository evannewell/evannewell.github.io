About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Economics at Texas A&M University. My research interests are in industrial organization, antitrust, and applied microeconomics, with a particular focus on how competition, market structure, and regulation affect firm behavior and consumer outcomes.

My research studies competition and firm behavior across a variety of industries. My current projects focus on a large merger in the U.S. banking industry and consolidation in the transportation and logistics sector.

Prior to my Ph.D., I earned an M.S. in Economics from Tufts University and a B.S. in Chemical Engineering from the University of Tennessee, Knoxville.
